THE RIVER

Frank's Poker Principles

───
  • First god. Second odds. Last cards.
  • Playing is about right cards with right board at right time with right people. Not for winning.
  • Observe then either hold or act. No desire.

Why No Desire?

The result depends on two things beyond your control:

  1. The luck distribution (god's river)
  2. How well that luck is used

Two ways to lose:

  • Miss the luck when it is there
  • Overplay when there is no luck

Other players' results are determined by their luck distribution — almost irrelevant to you. No desire means no desire from cards, chips, ranks, or anything else.

The Practice

Poker is about seeking certainty from uncertainty. When you need to reset, recite:

"Fear and greed both distort stats. Cleaning up greed by thinking: I walk in the randomness river set up by god, taking a piece from it only. Already took some? Should I fold now? Am I greedy? Cleaning up fear by thinking of Minglan. Am I having fear? Then review quantum analysis."

Quantum Analysis

Every player is a quantum. They move with the force of random power.

Quantum Types

Scan each opponent:

  • Good cards hit or miss
  • All cards low cost hit or miss
  • Chasing with hope
  • Mixed play (bluffing to confuse)
  • Calling station without sense of value
  • Calling station with sense of value

Quantum States (Fear / Greed Matrix)

Fear Greed Strategy
High Low Fold most. Bluff when possible.
Low Low Call when feeling. Take it easy. Bluff when possible.
Low High When leading, hit hard.
High High When leading, hit hard. May overbet.

Connectivity

  • Good cards don't mean good connectivity.
  • Connected quantum is dangerous.
  • Call hands with disconnected quantum.
  • Bluff the conservative. Catch the aggressive ones' bluff.
  • Tangled with disconnected quantum: squeeze, check-raise. Make it personal.
  • Taking personal is a sign. Losing the check-raise is a sign. Short-stack push is a sign.
  • Quantum tangle is a temporary state.

Bluffing

  • Bluff the disconnected conservative.
  • Catch the bluff from disconnected aggressive.
  • Catching one bluff → the next one is real cards. People mix their plays.
  • Illusion: catching a bluff creates a feeling of overpowering. Stay grounded.

Squeezing

  • Obscures the water. Opponents can't read your offense.
  • Takes chips when everyone folds.
  • Choose timing and audience carefully.
  • Prepare to fold when called.
  • Creates personal tension — exploitable on disconnected loose quantum.
  • Won't work on a very active table.

Attack Patterns

  • Attack like a leopard — unexpected, careful, watchful.
  • Every group attack must mix bluff and real cards.
  • Attack in a grouped way like a rolling snowball.
  • Bigger group attacks need the god's support. Watch the god.
  • Squeezing is the turbo pump for lucky hands — not a chip-winning move by itself.

Signs from God

There are signs god tells you to raise. There are signs you need to fold — not because of your cards, but because of the distribution of chances. There are signs you need to call. God is fair to each quantum.

The worst failures happen when a quantum forgets the random god has the ultimate power. Not the cards. Not emotions.

Observation of signs is the most important thing.

Reminders

  • When human is thinking, god is laughing. Observation > thinking.
  • Seeking luck is wrong. Seeking connection is the goal.
  • Good cards are not good connectivity.
  • Disruptions: good cards + good flop. Feel of being the victim. Context-switching to survival mode (animal instinct).
  • When you win a hand, feel lucky. Thank the random god for what you were given.
  • Ego stays inside the boat of self-awareness. God is always overpowering.
  • Don't play the god. Yourself is not god. Don't play the hot players. Don't chase the draws.
  • Don't play the cards. Play the human. Appreciate the god.
