Today's Strategy Tip — June 11, 2026
bankroll/session discipline

The hardest poker skill isn't reading opponents, calculating odds, or bluffing. It's knowing when to leave. You've had the session: up 3 buy-ins, then a bad beat, then a cooler, then you're down 1 buy-in and grinding, and before you know it, you're 3 hours past your planned stop time and down 4 buy-ins. This isn't bad luck — it's a structural failure of your exit protocol.

The fix: decouple the decision to leave from your emotional state. Set two hard exit triggers BEFORE you sit down — a time trigger and a loss trigger. Time: 2 hours, hard stop. Loss: 1.5 buy-ins, hard stop. Whichever hits first. No negotiation. No 'one more orbit.' The key insight from your own philosophy — No desire — applies here directly: the desire to 'win it back' is exactly the same greed that plays junk to showdown at 8% win rate. It's desire distorting the quantum.

But the most powerful layer: set a WIN exit too. Up 2 buy-ins? Cash out and leave. The dopamine from winning is just as dangerous as the cortisol from losing — it loosens your preflop standards, makes you feel invincible, and leads to the exact same collapse pattern. The Recite says 'Fear and greed both distort stats.' The win exit protects you from greed. The loss exit protects you from fear. Walk away at either trigger. The god will still be there tomorrow. This session is one sample in the river of time — don't let a single sample rewrite your strategy.
