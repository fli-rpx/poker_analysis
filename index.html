positional

The blinds are a tax you pay for sitting at the table — but they are also a weapon when you have position. When the action folds to you on the button or cutoff, you are holding the highest ground. Attack like a leopard: unexpected, careful, watchful. A well-timed steal isn't about the cards; it's about reading the quantum state of the blinds. Are they fearful? Greedy? Disconnected?

First God, Second Odds, Last Cards. The god of position whispers to you when the blinds are weak. A small raise here prints money without ever seeing a flop. But watch the signs — if a short-stack pushes back, that is a sign from god to fold. Don't play the cards. Play the human. Appreciate the god.
